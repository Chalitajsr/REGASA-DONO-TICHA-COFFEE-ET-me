Looking after the forest that grows our coffee.

For us this is simply how the coffee is grown. It is not an extra program.

The forest stays

Our coffee grows under native shade trees. We do not clear forest to plant more.

Every lot can be traced

Each lot is tracked from its plot and washing station through to export, so you can follow your bag back to where it grew.

We buy direct

We buy from farmers and sell to roasters with no chain of middlemen in between.

Washing close to the farm

Our washing stations sit near the farms, so cherry is processed fresh and quality stays in local hands.

Seedlings for our farmers

Each season we supply partner farmers with young coffee seedlings, so they can replace old trees without carrying the whole cost themselves.

Trust built over decades

After more than 45 years on the same land, many farmers sell only to us, and they did so long before any contract asked them to.
