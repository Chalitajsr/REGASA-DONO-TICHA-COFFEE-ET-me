Coffee from our family's forest in Anfilo.

We grow, wash and dry coffee in the Anfilo area of Nekemte, Western Ethiopia, and we sell it straight to roasters. Anfilo is where our founder Regasa was born.

Nekemte (Anfilo area) · Western Ethiopia

Regasa has been around coffee in Anfilo since he was a boy.

He was born in Anfilo and learned coffee from his family. In 1980 he started his own farm. His wife Sekuare worked next to him through every harvest, and today their children work in the business too.

  • 150 hectares that we farm ourselves
  • 100+ farmers nearby who sell to us
  • Since 1980 on the same land
