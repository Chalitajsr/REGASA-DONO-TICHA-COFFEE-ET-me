Regasa Dono Ticha, Anfilo

Born in the forest he still farms.

Regasa Dono Ticha was born in Anfilo into a coffee family. As a boy he followed his parents through the shade trees at harvest time and learned coffee the way most children here do, by doing it.

In 1980 he started his own plot. His wife, Sekuare Berkesa, worked beside him through every harvest, long before RDT had a name. Today their children work in the business with them.

Over the years neighbouring farmers started selling to us too. We now farm 150 hectares of our own and buy from more than 100 smallholder farmers nearby.

Coffee has grown here for a very long time.

Our coffee is sold as Nekemte and grown in the Anfilo area, in the forest belt of western Ethiopia. This is the part of the world where Arabica coffee grew wild before anyone planted it. Families around Anfilo have grown coffee here for roughly thirteen generations, about four hundred years. Regasa is one more link in that chain.

How we got here

1980

The first plot

Regasa starts his own coffee plot in the Anfilo forest, on inherited land, working the shaded slopes by hand next to neighbouring farmers.

1996

The first washing station

We build a small community wet mill so cherry can be processed close to where it grows. Quality and traceability stay in the family's hands.

2009

The first direct export

We ship our first container straight to an overseas roaster, skipping the domestic auction for the first time.

2016

The children join

Regasa and Sekuare's children take on cupping, logistics and the relationships with buyers. RDT Coffee becomes an export house with its own quality control.

Today

Roasters in five places

Roasters in South Africa, Germany, Hong Kong, Belgium and Italy buy from us. It is the same forest and the same family, with 150 hectares of our own and more than 100 farmers next door.
