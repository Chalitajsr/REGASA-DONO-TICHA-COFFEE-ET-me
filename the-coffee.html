Our coffee

Our coffee is Nekemte, grown in the Anfilo area of Western Ethiopia, where the family has farmed since 1980. It comes washed and natural.

Washed

Ripe cherry is pulped and washed at our washing station, then the coffee dries on raised beds. Usually a cleaner, brighter cup.

Natural

Whole ripe cherry dries slowly in the sun on raised beds, then is hulled. Usually a sweeter, fruitier cup.
